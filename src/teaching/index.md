## Mentoring

I often work with students who are learning to conduct research. I train and mentor research assistants on behavioral and psychophysiological studies, as well as other projects. I support students pursuing higher education, such as a PhD, PsyD, MD, JD, or master's, just as much as those pursuing another career.

My current [lab handbook](/resources/) is tailored primarily for research practicum students at Swarthmore College.
